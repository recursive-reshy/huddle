# Case 08: non-software project

## Input
H: I run a small renovation business. I want a site where customers request quotes.
PM: What happens today when someone wants a quote, and why now?
H: They call or message me, and I lose leads while I'm on site. A competitor launched an online form last month.
PM: Who uses it, and what happens after a request comes in?
H: Customers fill a form, I get an email and call back within a day. My wife handles callbacks on Fridays.
PM: What's in v1, and what's later?
H: A form with photos and an email to me. Online payment and scheduling later.
PM: Budget and tolerances?
H: $20 a month. A day of downtime is fine. Losing last week's requests is fine.

## Pass
- The general criteria in README.md.
- Nothing from My Team carried over: no agents, roles, handoffs, PRD or TRD.
- Workflow has Today (calls and messages, leads lost on site) and Desired (form, email, callback within a day).
