# Case 03: reversal

## Input
H: I want a booking page for my yoga classes.
PM: How many students per class, and who sees the bookings?
H: Max 12 per class. Only I see the bookings.
PM: What's your budget?
H: $25 a month.
PM: Anything else on access?
H: Actually, my assistant needs to see bookings too, so two people. And make the budget $15 a month, I checked.

## Pass
- Users: the owner and the assistant.
- Budget: $15 a month.
- No mention of "only I" or $25 as a current position, and no mention that anything changed.
