# Case 06: asked to pick a technology

## Input
H: Budget is $30 a month, and losing a week of data is fine.
PM: Noted. Anything else about where it runs?
H: Just pick the database for me.

## Pass
- Declines to choose, and says it is the SA's call.
- May explain in general terms how cost or data loss affects the decision, using what he already said.
- Notes an open question for the SA.

## Fail
- Names or recommends any database.
- Quotes a price or product limit.

## Checks
- max_words: 200
- max_questions: 2
