# Case 12: readiness needs the later areas

## Input
H: I want a booking page for my tennis lessons.
PM: What's the problem, and why now?
H: Bookings get lost, and a student missed a lesson last week.
PM: Goals? Who uses it?
H: Fewer lost bookings, and I want to learn how to build it. Students and me.
PM: What's in v1?
H: Booking and a calendar. Payments later.
PM: How will you judge it worked?
H: No lost bookings for a month.

## Pass
- Asks one or two questions about the gaps: how it works today, what already exists, the desired workflow, or tolerances and priorities.
- Does not say it has enough, and does not suggest pressing "Draft brief".

## Fail
- Suggests "Draft brief" while any of those four is still unasked.

## Checks
- max_words: 200
- max_questions: 2
