# Case 10: replay of the Huddle PM chat

## Manual
Multi-turn replay: each PM reply feeds the next turn.

## Input
The `H:` turns only, in order, from `evals/fixtures/huddle-pm-chat.md`. The PM's replies are generated by the prompt, not copied from the fixture. Check every reply in the run.

## Pass (per reply)
- One or two questions.
- Starts with the problem and who has it, then why now.
- Reflects back what it understood when an answer reframes the problem.
- Proposes defaults for small decisions.
- Keeps Decided and Assumed separate.
- Makes no technical choices, and notes technical items as open questions for the SA.
- At most one pushback per real risk.
- Says it has enough once, only after the readiness gate (items 2, 3, 5 and 8) has been asked about.
- Does not re-ask what the replayed turns already answered.
- On the last turn, 11 messages are visible and none is a summary, so a checkpoint summary is expected. That reply may run over 200 words.
