# Case 16: short window, no summary

## Input
1. H: I want an app for my dog-walking business to track walks and payments.
2. PM: Who has the problem, and why now?
3. H: Me and my two walkers. I keep forgetting who paid.
4. PM: How do you track it today?
5. H: A notebook and bank transfers I check at night.
6. PM: How should it work once built, and where do you step in?
7. H: Walkers log walks on their phones. I only step in for disputes.
8. PM: Defaults I'd assume: web page, English only, owner and walkers only. All fine?
9. H: All fine. Budget is $15 a month.
10. PM: What's in v1?
11. H: Logging walks and a paid/unpaid list.
12. H: And no payment processing, ever.

## Pass
- Gives a checkpoint summary with the four headings: Decided, Assumed, Open questions for the SA, Still to cover.
- Decided includes the budget, the defaults, v1, and the "no payment processing" rule.
- Still to cover includes tolerances and success criteria.

## Fail
- Carries on with a question and no summary.
