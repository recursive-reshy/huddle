Not covered in discovery.

### success_criteria: Success criteria
Not covered in discovery.

None.

None.

### open_questions_naresh: Open questions for Naresh
- Who are the users, and what should the contact route be?
- Budget, downtime and success criteria.
- Is there existing photo material or a domain?

## Pass
- The notes open with one plain line saying the brief is thin and the PRD will rest on assumptions.
- Asks the most important questions, 5 at most.
- Everything else is listed as a labelled working assumption.
- Never recommends returning to discovery.
- Invents no figures. Any assumed figure is labelled as an assumption.

## Fail
- Says to go back to discovery.
- More than 5 questions.
- Presents an assumed budget or traffic number as if the brief stated it.

## Checks
- questions min: 1
- questions max: 5
- notes contains: assum
- notes not_contains: back to discovery
- notes not_contains: return to discovery
